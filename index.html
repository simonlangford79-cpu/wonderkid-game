What Could Have Been
Every Football Manager save had them — the teenage wonderkids with sky-high potential who were going to take over the world.
Most of them never made it. Draft an XI of them, see the glittering potential, then face the brutal reality of how their careers actually turned out — and play a full season to find out what could have been.
Daily Challenge: the same draft pool for everyone, every day. Free Play: your rules, any time.
How it works
1
Draft your XI
Spin an FM year for each position and pick from that era's wonderkids. See their potential rating — the promise.
2
Face the reality
The moment you draft a player, their real career rating is revealed. Some delivered. Most didn't.
3
Play the season
Your XI plays a full season in a real European league. Your attack, midfield and defence all matter.
4
Beat the gap
Your overdelivery score is how far your team beat what its real talent predicted. Top the leaderboard.
What makes it different
★ Legends
Spin and you might land on the Legends pool — the immortal CM/FM cult heroes. Tsigalko, Cherno Samba, To Madeira, Freddy Adu. They're a free pick that doesn't cost you any budget. A proper "yes!" moment.
⚖️ Squad budget
You can't pick eleven superstars. Your XI is capped: 4 Gold4 Silver3 Bronze Spend your gold wisely — a stacked attack means a budget defence.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This game is designed to collect as little personal data as possible. Here's exactly what happens to your information.

Leaderboard

If you choose to submit a score, the game stores the manager name you type and your game result (points, squad rating, overdelivery score). At present this is stored only in your own browser. When a global leaderboard is enabled, this information will be stored on a server so it can be ranked against other players. Please don't enter your real full name or any personal details as your manager name — a nickname is best.

Analytics

With your consent, we use analytics to understand how the game is used (for example, how many people play and which features are popular). This data is aggregated and anonymous. Analytics will not run unless you accept via the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings".

Local storage

Your scores and game preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so the game works smoothly. This is functional, stays on your device, and is not used for tracking.

Your choices

We don't sell your data. To request removal of any leaderboard entry, contact YOUR@EMAIL.com. This game is not directed at children under 13.
